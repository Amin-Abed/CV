Biostatistics — Spatial & Bayesian Methods

Amin Abed

Postdoctoral Fellow, Department of Community Health and Epidemiology, Dalhousie University Faculty of Medicine. Working at the intersection of Bayesian spatial statistics and epidemiology, with a continuing interest in the theory behind the methods.

Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada Amin.Abed@Dal.ca English · Persian (native) · French (basic)

Research

01

My work sits between spatial statistical theory and its application to population health — small area estimation, spatiotemporal modelling of infectious disease transmission, and the sampling and missing-data problems that arise when disease surveillance data are incomplete or irregular.
